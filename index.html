My Sarauniya, I keep thinking about how ordinary the beginning looked from the outside. Two course mates who didn't really know each other. A group assignment. Me asking to take pictures of your notes because I hadn't copied mine. Then your message about your bag being stolen. I wanted you to stay calm, and I told you that God knows best. At the time, neither of us could have known how much that small conversation would come to mean to me. Life is full of loud, dramatic moments, but some of the most important things arrive quietly. Your encouragement when I needed to study. The way you care about the little things. The way you can make me feel supported without needing to make a big announcement about it. I don't want to promise you a future as if I can control every page of it. I want to meet the future honestly. If Allah wills, may whatever is written for us grow with patience, mercy, understanding and barakah. May we learn to care for each other better, especially when life gets difficult. Please remember this: you don't have to do something extraordinary every day to be precious. You don't have to be cheerful all the time, or have everything figured out. You are allowed to be human. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to need care, too. I made this little impossible thing because I wanted today's gift to feel different. But even all these effects, lights and words have a limit. They can show you something pretty; they can't measure what you mean to me. So here's the simplest truth I can give you: I'm grateful that I got to know you. I'm grateful that you're real. And I'm glad that, among all the people I could have met that day, our paths crossed. With love, and with hope for whatever good tomorrow brings,
